About the Film

Heritage is a quiet family drama set almost entirely inside one Malaysian-Chinese family home, over the final stretch of a dying patriarch's life and his funeral. Underneath, it's about the unequal weight of caregiving, the things that go unsaid in a family, and what actually gets inherited when a parent dies — not money, but duty, silence, and old wounds.

Based on true events and inspired by the director's own family, the film holds two real outcomes in the same frame — a permanent fracture and a reunion. It is an intimate, autobiographical story told through details that are unmistakably Malaysian-Chinese.

"A story about family, responsibility, and the weight of love." — Fong Ky Lee, Director

Our Story

MicroCrew Productions didn't start with a studio deal, an investor, or a mentor in the industry. It started with a question: what happens if we just start anyway?

We are an independent film production company based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. But what we're building reaches far beyond where we're based. Because the problem we're trying to solve isn't a Malaysian problem — it's a global one.

"Talent has no borders. But opportunity does. We're here to change that."

Somewhere right now there is a director who has never been given a camera. An actor who has never been given a script. A writer whose stories have never been produced. Not because they lack talent — but because nobody gave them the door to walk through. MicroCrew exists to be that door.

Our mission is to find undiscovered creative talent — directors, actors, writers, cinematographers, storytellers of every kind — and give them their first real opportunity. Not as a charity. As a genuine belief that the best stories in the world are still untold, and the people who need to tell them just haven't had their chance yet.

"We're not just making films. We're building the careers of the people who will define what film looks like next."

Heritage is the first proof of that. Fong Ky Lee is a director who had the vision and the craft but needed a production company willing to back her. MicroCrew was that company. And Heritage is what happened when we said yes.

We are starting from Kuala Lumpur. But we are building for everywhere. If you have a story that needs to be told, or you know someone who does — we want to hear it.

"Never give up. Take it slow. You have time."

— MicroCrew Productions
